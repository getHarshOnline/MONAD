\subsection{Protocol-record correspondence}\label{ss:profile-correspondence}

Fix a carrier $X$, admitted protocol maps $T_k:X\to Z$, and
record maps $p_i:Z\to O$. For the probabilistic profile, $O$ is
the specified space of record distributions. Let
$F_{k,i}=p_i\circ T_k$.
\leandef{MonadFormal.Profile.recordFamily}

\begin{proposition}[Protocol-record correspondence]
\label[proposition]{prop:profile-correspondence}
The fixed-context schema instantiated by these protocols and records has
$x\approx_F y$ exactly when every admitted protocol-record pair
assigns $x$ and $y$ the same record distribution. Any fixed decision
rule applied to those distributions preserves their equality.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Unfold the definition of $F$: its indices are pairs $(k,i)$ and its
values are $p_i(T_k(x))$. Equality at every index is precisely the
stated protocol-record condition. Applying a function to equal
distributions gives equal outputs.
\leanproof{MonadFormal.Profile.record_equivalence,MonadFormal.Profile.decision_rules_preserve_indist}
\end{proof}

For this profile, $C$ is determined by the regulator and resources,
$X=\StateSpace$, records compose coarse-graining and the record
channel, and protocols are those admitted by the full protocol contract.
