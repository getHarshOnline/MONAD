\subsection{Exact correspondence with partition abstraction}

The budgeted relation is not automatically new mathematics. For
partition abstraction, the correspondence below is exact.

For $q:X\to Y$ and $S\subseteq X$, set
$\alpha_q(S)=q[S]$, $\gamma_q(U)=q^{-1}[U]$ and
$\operatorname{Sat}_q(S)=\gamma_q\alpha_q(S)$.
For an action $T:X\to X$, write $\operatorname{Post}_T(S)=T[S]$.
\leandef{MonadFormal.saturate,MonadFormal.abstractPost}

\begin{theorem}[Partition backward-completeness criterion]
\label{thm:partition-completeness}
For $q:X\to Y$, $r:X\to Z$ and $T:X\to X$, the equality
\[
 \alpha_r\operatorname{Post}_T(S)
  =\alpha_r\operatorname{Post}_T\operatorname{Sat}_q(S)
 \quad\text{for every }S\subseteq X
\]
holds if and only if
$q(x)=q(y)\Rightarrow r(Tx)=r(Ty)$ for all $x,y$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For necessity, take $S=\{x\}$ and $q(x)=q(y)$.
Then $y\in\operatorname{Sat}_q(S)$; equality of the two images
forces $r(Ty)=r(Tx)$. For sufficiency, $S\subseteq
\operatorname{Sat}_q(S)$ gives one inclusion. For the other, every
$y$ in the saturation shares a $q$-fiber with some $x\in S$.
The assumed fiber condition gives $r(Ty)=r(Tx)$.
\leanproof{MonadFormal.backward_complete_iff}
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Budgeted backward completeness]
\label{cor:budget-completeness}
For an affordable action $a$, the preceding criterion holds with
$q=q_b$, $r=q_{b-c(a)}$ and $T=T_a$, where $q_b$ is the quotient
of \eqref{eq:budget-equivalence}.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Equality of $q_b$ values is equivalence at budget $b$. The residual-budget
law gives equivalence of the images at budget $b-c(a)$, which is equality
of their residual quotient values. Apply the criterion.
\leanproof{MonadFormal.budget_backward_complete,MonadFormal.Budget.indist_iff_family}
\end{proof}
This is an exact collision with the partition/powerset form of
backward completeness~\cite{giacobazzi2000making}. It does not
identify every abstract domain with a partition, nor establish an
optimal resource-bounded repair procedure.
