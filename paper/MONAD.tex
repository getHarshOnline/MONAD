\begin{abstract}
MONAD seeks a calculus of endogenous distinguishability: how a
mathematical system can define, compress, transport, and revise its own
space of distinctions.
The set-based starting point identifies exact sufficient quotients and
the obstruction to nontrivial compression under unrestricted future
distinctions. Resource-indexed continuation, obtainable repair,
statistical simulation and three distinct forms of temporal dependence
make the assumptions behind these comparisons explicit. A fixed-context
observer profile then connects state, records and intervention influence
to entropy and information bounds. The proved constructions are bounded
specializations; a general calculus of endogenous context change remains
the research problem.
\end{abstract}
